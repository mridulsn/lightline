How to play

  • Tap a square to place a mirror. Tap again to turn it, once more to clear it.

  • Light bounces off mirrors at right angles.

  • Light every crystal using the mirrors you have. That number is always the fewest that works.

Wall: stops light
Bolted mirror: fixed
Prism: splits light in two
Crystal: light passes through

Keys: arrows move, Space places or turns, Delete clears, U undoes.

About lightline

A new light puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Place mirrors so the laser lights every crystal. Monday is gentle; by the weekend there are prisms that split the light in two.

Each puzzle is made from its date, right in your browser. Every one is checked to be solvable with exactly the mirrors you're given, and never with fewer. Practice mode makes as many extra puzzles as you like.

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you log in. Your progress is kept on this device. If you log in with Google or Discord, your results, level and settings are also backed up to your account so they follow you to other devices. Delete account copy (in the account panel) removes that backup.
